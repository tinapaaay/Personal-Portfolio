The house rule

Since 2002 we have squeezed every lemon to order, picked our mint from the garden out back and poured each drink over ice we cut by hand. Nothing fancy. Just a good glass, made properly, for whoever walks in.

TheMenu

The Ritual

Every drink on the menu goes through the same four steps, in this order. It takes about ninety seconds from order to glass.

01
10 seconds

Muddle

Fresh mint and a spoon of cane sugar, pressed gently so the leaves bruise without turning bitter.

02
25 ml, every time

Squeeze

Lemons from the Carpinteria farmers' market, pressed when you order. Juice older than a few hours goes flat, so we don't keep any.

03
12 seconds, hard

Shake

Big cubes, short and hard. Enough to chill and dilute the drink by about a fifth.

04
Last touch

Garnish

A wheel of lemon and the top of a mint sprig, clapped once between the palms so it smells before you taste it.
