First section

Body text is Manrope in the softer article ink, left-aligned, never justified. Use curly quotes (“like this”), and wrap a URL in span.nowrap so it never breaks in two.

A bold lead-in. Use a short bold phrase to start a paragraph that covers one named item, like a project, an issue or a talk.

Second section

Section headings are Plus Jakarta Sans with a hairline above. They are larger than bold lead-ins, so the outline of the paper reads at a glance. The small plum capitals are kept for the one label above the title.

  • Use a list only for items that are truly parallel.
  • Otherwise, write in sentences.
